# 20 proven ways to increase yield & profit (indicative, India)

Most farms can raise yields 20–60% without new land — by fixing timing, spacing, seed, moisture and markets. Ordered roughly by ease.

## 1. Test your soil (free/cheap, biggest ROI)
- Soil Health Card: free testing via your agriculture department/KVK — gives N, P, K, organic carbon, pH, micronutrients.
- Fertilize by report, not habit: most farms over-apply N and under-apply P/K/micronutrients — correcting this alone adds 10–20% yield and cuts cost.

## 2. Use certified/quality seed of the right variety
- Certified seed vs farm-saved old seed: 10–25% yield gap. Replace/renew seed every 2–3 years; foundation seed for seed plots.
- Pick the variety for YOUR purpose (duration, grain type, disease resistance, market demand) — not just what neighbours grow.

## 3. Treat seed before sowing
- Fungicide (thiram/carboxin as per label) or Trichoderma 4–8 g/kg seed: prevents seedling wilt, smut, root rot.
- Rhizobium + PSB for legumes (gram, soybean, pea): free nitrogen + phosphorus unlock = 10–15% more yield at ~₹100/acre cost.

## 4. Sow on time — the cheapest yield booster
- Every week of delay after the optimum window costs roughly 1–1.5 quintal/acre in wheat, similar elsewhere. Plan labour/seed/machine 3 weeks before the window.

## 5. Line sowing / drill sowing at correct spacing
- Drill/zero-till sowing vs broadcasting: uniform plant population (+15–25% yield), 25% less seed, easier weeding & spraying.
- Maintain plant-to-plant spacing (thin where crowded) — crowding = weak plants, lodging, disease.

## 6. Split nitrogen doses; don't dump all at once
- Urea in 2–3 splits at critical stages (tillering/square/pod filling per crop) instead of one shot: +10% uptake, less leaching.

## 7. Irrigate at CRITICAL stages, not by calendar
- Each crop has 2–3 yield-deciding stages (e.g., wheat CRI 21 days; maize silking; groundnut pegging; tomato fruit set). Missing one costs 20–30%.
- AWD in paddy (irrigate when cracks show): −30% water, same yield, fewer greenhouse gases.

## 8. Drip/sprinkler where possible
- Drip: 35–50% water saving + 20–40% yield gain (vegetables, banana, sugarcane) + less disease. Micro-irrigation subsidy up to 45–55% under PMKSY — apply at your agri office.

## 9. Mulch
- Straw/plastic mulch: 25–40% less evaporation, weed control, cleaner produce. Sugarcane trash mulch between rows; plastic mulch for vegetables & chilli.

## 10. Weed-free critical window (20–45 days)
- Weeds in the first 6 weeks eat 30–40% of your fertilizer. One timely weeding/inter-cultivation at 20–25 days = the single cheapest yield gain in rainfed crops.

## 11. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) — stop calendar spraying
- Prevent: resistant varieties, clean fields, bird perches (40/acre), pheromone traps (2–4/acre), yellow/blue sticky traps, light traps.
- Monitor: scout twice a week; spray only at threshold, at the right stage (eggs/larvae), rotate chemical groups, use label dose. Saves money AND protects bees/pollinators.

## 12. Micronutrients & foliar feeds
- Zinc (cereals), sulphur (oilseeds/onion), boron (cotton/groundnut), magnesium — cheap foliars at flowering can return 10× their cost on deficient soils (see soil report).

## 13. FYM/compost/green manure every season
- 8–10 t/acre FYM or green manure (dhaincha/sunhemp 45 days before sowing): rebuilds organic carbon, water holding, and makes fertilizer work better.

## 14. Intercropping & relay cropping
- Pair a tall and short crop with different root depths: cotton+blackgram, sugarcane+potato/onion, maize+cowpea, banana+ginger. Same acre, 20–40% more total income + risk spread.

## 15. Crop rotation
- Legume after cereal breaks pest/disease cycles and leaves 25–40 kg N/acre in soil for free. Never repeat solanaceous (potato-tomato-chilli) on the same field within 3 years.

## 16. Laser land leveling (custom-hire)
- ₹600–800/acre custom hire: saves 20–25% water, uniform germination, 8–12% yield gain. Share machine with neighbours.

## 17. Timely harvest & careful threshing
- Over-ripe harvest = shattering losses up to 5–7%; wet threshing = discoloured grain (lower price). Harvest at correct moisture, morning hours.

## 18. Post-harvest is half the profit
- Grade, clean, dry to correct moisture, pack properly: farmers routinely gain 8–15% price just by cleaning & grading before sale (see post_harvest_marketing.md).

## 19. Sell smart: MSP floor, market ceiling, AgriEasy reach
- Know the current MSP (floor price) before selling; check eNAM & AgriEasy live prices; avoid distress sale right at harvest peak if you can store; aggregate with neighbours for bulk-lot buyers.

## 20. Keep simple records & repeat what works
- Notebook/app per field: variety, dates, inputs, costs, yield, sale price, buyer. Within 2 seasons you'll see exactly which practice pays on YOUR soil — that's real farming intelligence.
