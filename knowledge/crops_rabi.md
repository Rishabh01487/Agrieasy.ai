# Rabi field crops — sowing-to-selling guide (indicative, India)

All doses/numbers are general recommendations; confirm with your local KVK/agriculture officer and soil health card. Pesticides: always follow the label.

## Wheat (Gehun)
- Season: Rabi; optimum sowing **Nov 1–20** (till Nov 30 with late-sown varieties). 120–140 day crop.
- Soil & land prep: Loam/clay loam; fine seedbed with 2 harrowings; zero-till drill into rice residue saves cost.
- Seed rate & spacing: 40 kg/acre timely sown (100 kg/ha); 50–60 kg/acre late sown; line sowing 20 cm rows at 4–5 cm depth. **Treat seed** with carboxin/thiram (label dose) against loose smut & termites.
- Irrigation: 4–6 irrigations at **CRI (21 days — most critical)**, tillering (40–45), jointing (60), flowering (80), milk (95), dough (105). Each irrigation ≈ 5–6 cm.
- Fertilizer (per acre, soil-test based): 48:24:24 NPK basal; top-dress 45 kg urea at first irrigation (21 days). Zinc 25 kg/ha once in 3 years if deficient.
- Weeds: Clodinafop/sulfosulfuron/metsulfuron as per label at 30–35 days (2–4 leaf stage of weeds) + one broadleaf-specific if needed.
- Pests: Termites (seed treatment; chlorpyriphos as per label in patches), armyworm occasionally, yellow rust.
- Diseases: Yellow/brown rust (yellow stripes on leaves — treat immediately as per label; grow resistant varieties), loose smut, Karnal bunt (avoid excess irrigation at flowering).
- Harvest: Grains hard, golden, ~20% moisture or when 85% ears ripe (110–120 days late varieties). Morning harvest reduces shattering.
- Yield: Average 16–20 q/acre; good management 22–24 q/acre (5.5–6 t/ha).
- Post-harvest: Dry to 10–12% moisture; store in metal bins/hermetic bags with neem leaves; grade & clean before sale.
- Marketing: Sell at MSP procurement centres when mandi < MSP (check each season's MSP), or hold 2–4 months in dry storage for lean-season price; list lots ≥10 q on AgriEasy for bulk flour-mill buyers.

## Mustard / Rapeseed (Sarson)
- Season: Rabi; sow **Oct 10 – Nov 5** with onset of cool weather. 110–140 days.
- Soil & land prep: Loam/light soil, well-drained; fine firm seedbed; 2 harrowings + planking.
- Seed rate & spacing: 1.5–2 kg/acre (tiny seed — mix with sand for even broadcast or drill in 45 cm lines at 2–3 cm depth); thin at 15–20 days to 10–15 cm plant spacing.
- Irrigation: 2–3: branching (30–35 days), flowering (60–65 — critical), siliqua filling (85–90). Avoid waterlogging.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 24:12:12 NPK + **20 kg sulphur/acre** (mustard loves S — big response in oil content); ½ N basal, ½ at first irrigation; FYM 5 t improves yield.
- Weeds: 1 hand weeding/inter-cultivation at 20–25 days; pendimethalin pre-emergence as per label for large fields.
- Pests: **Mustard aphid** (main pest — check tops weekly Dec–Jan; yellow sticky traps; ladybirds; spray as per label at threshold), painted bug (early & harvest stage — clean field bunds).
- Diseases: Alternaria blight, white rust (remove stag-heads; avoid dense sowing), downy mildew.
- Harvest: 75% siliquae yellow-brown, seeds rattle (105–120 days). Harvest morning to avoid shattering; stack 5–6 days to mature before threshing.
- Yield: Average 4–6 q/acre; good 8–10 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry to 8% moisture; store in gunny bags on pallets; oil extraction raises value ~40%.
- Marketing: Strong oil-mill demand; sell at harvest or after 2–3 months; AgriEasy bulk listings to oil mills fetch mandi+ prices without mandi fees.

## Chickpea / Gram (Chana)
- Season: Rabi; sow **mid-Oct to mid-Nov** (soil residual moisture). 95–115 days.
- Soil & land prep: Medium black/loam, well-drained (gram hates waterlogging); 1 ploughing + 2 harrowings.
- Seed rate & spacing: 30–35 kg/acre; rows 30–45 cm × 10 cm plant-to-plant, 5–8 cm depth. **Treat seed** with Rhizobium + PSB + Trichoderma.
- Irrigation: Light irrigation at branching (30–35 days); second light at pod filling (75–80 days) if dry. Over-watering = excess vegetative growth + wilt.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 8:16:8 NPK starter + 20 kg sulphur; foliar 2% DAP at pod filling boosts yield ~10%.
- Weeds: 1 weeding at 20–25 days + 1 at 45 days; gram covers ground fast after.
- Pests: **Pod borer (Helicoverpa)** — key pest: pheromone traps/acre, bird perches 40/acre, HaNPV spray, hand-pick larvae early morning; chemical only at threshold (1–2 larvae/meter row) as per label. Cutworm in seedling stage.
- Diseases: Wilt (grow resistant varieties; 3-year rotation; avoid early sowing in warm soil), Ascochyta blight, dry root rot.
- Harvest: Plants yellow-brown, pods dry & rattle, seeds hard (95–110 days). Morning harvest; dry 3–4 days; thresh.
- Yield: Average 4–6 q/acre; good 8–10 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry to 9–10% moisture; store in PICS/hermetic bags (weevil-prone); grading by size fetches premium.
- Marketing: High dal demand; sell at MSP centres or post-season premium; kabuli chana gets nearly double price — AgriEasy direct-to-dal-mill listings work well.

## Potato (Aalu)
- Season: Rabi; plant **Oct 15 – Nov 30** (plains). 90–110 days.
- Soil & land prep: Sandy loam best; fine loose tilth — 2 ploughings + 2 harrowings; ridges 50–60 cm apart. **Use certified seed tubers** (50–60 g pieces, 2–3 eyes, sprouted); treat as per label against black scurf.
- Seed rate & spacing: 800–1,000 kg/acre seed; 50–60 × 20 cm on ridges, 6–8 cm deep.
- Irrigation: Light frequent: every 7–10 days; **critical at stolon initiation (20–25 days) and tuber development (45–70)**; stop 10 days before harvest; avoid moisture swings (deformed/shatter-cracked tubers).
- Fertilizer (per acre): 40–48:24:36 NPK (half N basal, half at earthing-up 30 days) + FYM 10 t; potassium & phosphorus drive tuber fill; magnesium foliar if needed.
- Weeds: Earthing-up at 30 days controls weeds + greening; metribuzin pre-emergence as per label for large farms.
- Pests: Aphids (virus vectors — monitor, act at threshold), cutworm (seedling), white grub in some zones.
- Diseases: **Late blight** (most destructive — cloudy humid spells Dec–Jan; preventive sprays as per label at first warning from KVK), early blight, black scurf, potato virus Y (use certified seed).
- Harvest: Vines yellow & die back (90–110 days); cut vines 10 days earlier; dig when soil dry; cure in shade 10–15 days (skin hardens).
- Yield: Average 90–120 q/acre; good 150–200 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Grade by size; no cuts/bruises/green tubers; store in dark cold stores (10–12°C) — green/sprouted potatoes lose price & safety; never store with onions (mutual spoilage).
- Marketing: Prices swing 3× within a year — cold storage + staggered sale is the profit strategy; AgriEasy listings of graded 50 kg bags to wholesale/retail buyers all season.

## Onion (Pyaaz)
- Season: Rabi main crop: nursery **Oct–Nov**, transplant **Dec–Jan**, harvest **Mar–May**. (Kharif & late-kharif in some regions.)
- Soil & land prep: Well-drained loam, pH 6.5–7.5; fine tilth; raised beds for nursery; main field flat beds 3×2 m or broad-bed-furrow.
- Seed rate & spacing: 4–5 kg/acre seed → nursery; transplant 45–60 day seedlings at 15×10 cm.
- Irrigation: Light frequent (10–12 days); **critical at bulb development (60–90 days after transplant)**; stop 10–15 days before harvest for storage quality; excess water = thick-necked bulbs & rot.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 32:16:32 NPK + 20–40 kg sulphur (S = pungency & storage life) + FYM 10 t; ½ N basal, ½ at 30–45 days; stop N after 60 days.
- Weeds: Onions can't compete — 2–3 hand weedings; oxyfluorfen pre-emergence as per label for large areas.
- Pests: **Thrips** (silvery streaks — blue sticky traps; spray as per label at threshold), onion maggot in some zones.
- Diseases: Purple blotch & Stemphylium blight (treat as per label at first spots), basal rot (crop rotation).
- Harvest: 50–70% neck fall / tops bending (100–130 days after transplant); cure 3–5 days in field with tops covering bulbs.
- Yield: Average 90–120 q/acre; good 160–200 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: THE crop where post-harvest decides profit: cure fully, cut tops 2 cm, grade (55–75 mm export grade), store in ventilated bamboo/net structures; Agrifound Light-red etc. store months.
- Marketing: Staggered sale Apr–Dec beats distress sale at harvest (prices often double by Sept–Oct); AgriEasy graded-lot listings reach urban wholesale & exporters directly.
