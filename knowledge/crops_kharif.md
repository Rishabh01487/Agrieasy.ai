# Kharif & annual field crops — sowing-to-selling guide (indicative, India)

All doses/numbers are general recommendations; confirm with your local KVK/agriculture officer and soil health card. Pesticides: always follow the label.

## Paddy / Rice (Dhaan)
- Season: Kharif (sow June 15 – July 15); shorter rabi/boro crop Jan–Feb in eastern & southern India.
- Soil & land prep: Fine puddled soil; 2 ploughings + puddling with rotavator/cultivator; level field for uniform water. Keep bunds leak-proof.
- Seed rate & spacing: 12–16 kg/acre for transplanting hybrids/direct-seeded (DRS) 20–25 kg/acre; transplant 20×15 cm (2–3 seedlings/hill at 20–25 days age).
- Irrigation: Keep saturated/2–3 cm standing water till panicle initiation; then **Alternate Wetting & Drying** — irrigate when field cracks appear (saves ~30% water, no yield loss); stop 10–15 days before harvest.
- Fertilizer (per acre, soil-test based): 48:24:24 NPK + 5 kg ZnSO₄ as basal; top-dress ½ N at tillering (20–25 days) and ¼ N at panicle initiation.
- Weeds: Pre-emergence pretilachlor (with safener, as per label) in DRS; or 1 hand-weeding + cono-weeder at 20–25 days.
- Pests: Yellow stem borer (dead hearts — clip & destroy, use Trichocards 3×/season), Brown plant hopper (drain water; insecticide as per label), leaf folder.
- Diseases: Blast (leaf/neck — avoid excess N, treat as per label), sheath blight, bacterial leaf blight.
- Harvest: 80% grains golden & hard (28–35 days after flowering); drain field 10–15 days earlier. Delay = shattering losses.
- Yield: Average 18–22 q/acre paddy; good management 26–30 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry to 12–14% moisture (2–3 days sun); mill within weeks or store in hermetic bags.
- Marketing: Sell paddy at MSP procurement centres (check current MSP) or sell aromatic/better varieties at premium on AgriEasy; milled rice fetches more — tie up with local miller.

## Maize (Makka)
- Season: Kharif (late June–mid July), rabi (Oct–Nov, higher yield), spring (Feb) in north.
- Soil & land prep: Well-drained loam; 1 ploughing + 2 harrowings; ridge/flat sowing; make field weed-free before sowing.
- Seed rate & spacing: 8–10 kg/acre; 60×20 cm (kharif), 60×25 cm (rabi hybrids).
- Irrigation: Critical: knee-high (V8), tasseling/silking (most critical — never stress), grain filling. Kharif usually rain-fed; rabi needs 4–6 irrigations.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 48:24:24 NPK basal; ½ N at knee-high, ¼ N at tasseling.
- Weeds: Atrazine pre-emergence (as per label) + 1 inter-cultivation at 20–25 days.
- Pests: **Fall armyworm** (check whorls weekly; sand+ash in whorl, NPV/Emamectin as per label), stem borer.
- Diseases: Turcicum leaf blight, maydis leaf blight; grow resistant hybrids.
- Harvest: Grains hard & glossy, black layer at base, husks dry (usually 90–110 days kharif).
- Yield: Average 20–25 q/acre; good 30–35 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry cobs to 12% moisture; shell; store with proper drying or sell quickly.
- Marketing: Strong demand from feed/starch/ethanol industry — sell via AgriEasy to bulk buyers or during lean months for better price.

## Cotton (Kapaa)
- Season: Kharif (May–June irrigated; June–July with monsoon).
- Soil & land prep: Deep black/medium soils; deep ploughing once in 3 years; ridges & furrows; treat seed against fungal wilt.
- Seed rate & spacing: Bt hybrids 1 packet (450 g) + refugia per acre; spacing 90–120 × 45–60 cm.
- Irrigation: First 60 days light irrigations; avoid waterlogging; protect squares/bolls during flowering (Aug–Sep); stop by mid-Oct for timely picking.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 48:24:24 NPK + 10 t FYM; split N at square formation & flowering; Mg/B foliar if deficiency seen.
- Weeds: Pendimethalin pre-emergence (as per label) + 2–3 inter-cultivations.
- Pests: **Pink bollworm** (install pheromone traps/acre from square initiation; destroy rosette flowers; follow pink bollworm action thresholds), whitefly/jassid (yellow sticky traps; neem oil), avoid repeated pyrethroid sprays.
- Diseases: Wilt/root rot (rotate crops), bacterial blight, leaf curl virus (control whitefly vector).
- Harvest: Pick fully burst bolls every 15–20 days, 3–4 pickings; keep clean & dry; avoid trash/moisture — cleanliness decides price.
- Yield: Average 6–8 q/acre lint; good 10–12 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry on clean tarpaulin; store cotton away from fuel/smell; grade by staple & cleanliness.
- Marketing: Sell to CCI procurement at MSP when market < MSP, or on AgriEasy to spinners/ginned lots; get lot ginned & baled for better realization.

## Soybean (Soya)
- Season: Kharif (June 20 – July 10 with monsoon onset).
- Soil & land prep: Well-drained medium black soil; 1 ploughing + 2 harrowings; broad-bed & furrow in high rainfall areas.
- Seed rate & spacing: 25–30 kg/acre; 30–45 × 5 cm line sowing (drill). **Treat seed** with Rhizobium + PSB culture.
- Irrigation: Mostly rain-fed; protective irrigation at pod filling (Sep) lifts yield sharply if dry spell.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 12:32:16 NPK (single basal dose at sowing); no N top dressing needed with good nodulation; sulphur 20 kg/acre on deficient soils.
- Weeds: Pendimethalin pre-emergence (as per label) + 1 weeding at 20–25 days.
- Pests: Girdle beetle (cut affected shoots), semilooper (NPV), tobacco caterpillar.
- Diseases: Rust (Sep-Oct humid spells — treat as per label early), yellow mosaic virus (grow resistant varieties; control whitefly), bacterial pustule.
- Harvest: Leaves yellow & drop, pods rattle, 85–90% pods brown (95–110 days). Morning harvest avoids shattering.
- Yield: Average 4–6 q/acre; good 8–10 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry to 8–10% moisture before storing (soy spoils fastest when damp).
- Marketing: Sell at harvest to oil mills/traders or hold 2–3 months in dry storage for post-season price rise; AgriEasy bulk-buyer listings work well for 50+ quintal lots.

## Sugarcane (Ganna)
- Season: Annual: Feb–March (spring, 12-month) or Oct–Nov (adsali ~15–18 months) in Maharashtra; ratoon crop follows for 1–2 years.
- Soil & land prep: Deep ploughing; trench method (150 cm) saves water & raises yield; treat setts with recommended fungicide-insecticide dip as per label.
- Seed rate & spacing: 4,000–5,000 three-bud setts/acre (~2.5–3 t seed); trenches 120–150 cm apart.
- Irrigation: Every 10–12 days summer, 15–20 winter; critical tillering (45–100 days) and grand growth (July–Oct); trash mulch in trenches cuts evaporation.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 10 t FYM + ~112:50:25 NPK; N in 3 splits (45, 90, 135 days); bio-compost/trash incorporation improves soil.
- Weeds: 2–3 inter-cultivations + trash mulch; atrazine pre-emergence as per label.
- Pests: Early shoot borer (setts treatment, earthing-up), top borer, pyrilla/woolly aphid (protect friendly insects).
- Diseases: Red rot (grow resistant varieties; remove infected clumps), wilt, smut (roughing).
- Harvest: At 10–11 month maturity (yellowing leaves, brix testing); cut & supply to mill within 24 hours — recovery falls after cutting.
- Yield: Average 280–320 q/acre; good trench-method 400–500 q/acre; ratoon 20–30% less.
- Post-harvest: No storage — supply chain is immediate; strip trash for extra cattle feed income.
- Marketing: Contract with sugar mill (statutory area); sell jaggery/gur route independently for premium in some regions.
