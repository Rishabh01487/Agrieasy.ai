## Why sell on AgriEasy
- Reach beyond your local mandi: bulk buyers, processors, retailers, exporters see your lot.
- Price discovery: see live/recent prices for your crop & grade before listing.
- Direct relationship: repeat buyers who know your quality; less middleman margin.
- Assured payment: platform-recorded payment is your proof and protection.

## Step 1 — Before you list: make the lot buyer-ready
1. Harvest at right maturity; dry/cure to correct moisture (grain snaps, not bends; soybean 8–10%, cereals 10–12%).
2. **Clean & grade** — remove dirt, broken/diseased units; sort by size for vegetables/onion. Graded lots sell faster at better prices.
3. Weigh accurately; keep your own tally. Decide lot size (bulk buyers like 50+ q grain lots; vegetables 5–20 q graded lots).
4. Know your floor price: current MSP (if applicable), mandi/eNAM rate, recent AgriEasy prices. Set target price + walk-away price.

## Step 2 — Photograph like a pro (phone is enough)
- Daylight, produce on a clean plain background (tarpaulin/sheet), no shadows.
- 3–5 photos: whole lot, close-up showing grain size/grade, one showing cleanliness/texture, one of weighing/grading.
- No filters, no misleading photos — disputes come back to you.

## Step 3 — Create the listing (be complete & honest)
- Crop + variety + grade (A/B or size class) + moisture if grain + quantity (quintals) + location (district) + harvest date + price (fixed or negotiable) + photos.
- Mention genuinely useful details: organic/near-organic practice, cold-stored potato, cured onion variety (storage type), poison-free (IPM) produce.

## Step 4 — Manage buyer conversations
- Reply same day; buyers move on in hours.
- Answer honestly on moisture, grade, chemicals used, storage. Over-promising kills repeat business and invites returns.
- Negotiate on price & quantity clearly in chat (keeps a record); agree delivery terms: who transports, where, when, who pays. **[CUSTOMIZE: in-app chat/negotiation features]**

## Step 5 — Payment: get paid safely
1. **Best: AgriEasy platform payment/escrow — [CUSTOMIZE: exact payment options & timelines].** Buyer pays through platform; money releases after delivery & weight confirmation. Safest for new buyer relationships.
2. UPI/bank transfer before dispatch for small orders, or part-advance (20–30%) + balance on delivery for trusted repeat buyers.
3. Cash only against signed receipt with amount, lot, weight, date, buyer phone.
4. Never hand over produce on "payment will come tomorrow" with an unknown buyer. No record = no recourse.
5. Report buyers who repeatedly cancel/dispute to platform support; rate honestly.

## Step 6 — Delivery & handover
- Weigh at pickup WITH the buyer present; both sign/approve the weight slip in-app.
- Photograph loaded vehicle & weight slip (30 seconds, saves disputes).
- Graded + well-packed produce survives transport — your rating depends on arrival condition.

## Step 7 — After the sale
- Confirm payment received in app/bank; save the record.
- Rate the buyer; message after a week: "Any feedback on quality?" — repeat buyers are the real income multipliers.
- Log sale in your farm record: date, grade, rate, buyer, payment mode.

## Smart selling patterns on AgriEasy
- **Stagger**: don't dump the whole harvest in glut week; list in lots across weeks/months if storage allows.
- **Aggregate**: combine with neighbours for bulk-lot size (50–100 q) to attract mill/export buyers; split fairly.
- **Grade ladder**: sell Grade A to premium buyers, B to processors — never mix grades in one lot.
- **Counter-season**: stored onion/potato/pulses listed in lean months (Sept–Dec) commonly fetch 30–100% over harvest-glut price.
- **Responsive + honest + repeat** beats one-off cleverness. Your rating is your brand.
