# Vegetable & fruit crops — sowing-to-selling guide (indicative, India)

All doses/numbers are general recommendations; confirm with your local KVK/horticulture officer. Pesticides: always follow the label.

## Tomato (Tamatar)
- Season: Year-round; main rabi crop nursery Sep–Oct, transplant Oct–Nov; spring-summer crop Jan–Feb with drip.
- Soil & land prep: Well-drained loam, pH 6–7; raised beds 60–90 cm or paired rows with drip; solarize nursery soil (clear polythene 4 weeks) to kill nematodes.
- Seed rate & spacing: 60–80 g/acre seed (nursery); transplant 40–50 cm × 30–45 cm (determinate) or 60×45 cm (staked semi-determinate); harden seedlings 5 days before transplant.
- Irrigation: Drip daily/alternate (best yields + 40% water saving); critical at flowering & fruit set; moisture swings = fruit cracking & blossom-end rot; avoid wetting foliage (disease).
- Fertilizer (per acre): 40:24:24 NPK + 10 t FYM; splits at transplant, 30, 45, 60 days; foliar Ca/B at flowering for crack-free fruit.
- Weeds: Mulching (silver-black plastic or straw) controls weeds + thrips + water loss.
- Pests: **Fruit borer (Helicoverpa)** — pheromone traps + HaNPV; leaf miner; whitefly (virus vector — yellow traps, net nursery).
- Diseases: Early & late blight (preventive sprays as per label in cloudy spells), **tomato leaf curl virus** (grow resistant hybrids; control whitefly; remove infected plants), bacterial wilt (rotate 3 years).
- Staking/pruning: Stake or string-train indeterminate types; prune suckers weekly for bigger uniform fruit.
- Harvest: Breaker stage (first colour) for long-distance market; deep red for local — pick every 3–4 days; morning cool hours.
- Yield: Average 80–120 q/acre; good drip+hybrid+staking 200–300 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: NEVER refrigerate below 10°C (loses taste); sort by colour & size; stack crates max 4 layers; grade A/B — grade A to premium buyers.
- Marketing: Pre-agree with traders/processing units before peak harvest; AgriEasy listings with grade+photos reach city retailers directly; process surplus into pulp/pickle with SHG.

## Chilli (Mirch)
- Season: Nursery Jun–Jul, transplant Jul–Aug (kharif); Feb transplant for summer crop with irrigation.
- Soil & land prep: Well-drained loam; raised beds; FYM 10 t/acre incorporated 3 weeks before.
- Seed rate & spacing: 200–250 g/acre seed (nursery 40–45 days); transplant 60×45 cm (dry chilli), 45×30 cm (green).
- Irrigation: Drip or alternate-row furrow every 8–12 days; critical at flowering & first picking; water stress = flower drop.
- Fertilizer (per acre): 24:16:16 NPK + FYM; ½ N basal, splits at 30/45/60 days; foliar micronutrient (Zn+B) at flowering for shine & pungency.
- Weeds: 2–3 hand weedings + mulch; pendimethalin as per label for large blocks.
- Pests: **Thrips (leaf curl upward)** — blue traps, acaricide-insecticide as per label, rotate groups; mites; fruit borers in wet spells.
- Diseases: Die-back/fruit rot (Colletotrichum — treat as per label at flowering), powdery mildew, Cercospora leaf spot; remove & burn infected twigs.
- Harvest: Green pickings start 70–80 days; for dry chilli pick fully red pods every 10–15 days (3–6 pickings); morning picking, keep dry.
- Yield: Average dry 4–6 q/acre (green 40–60 q); good dry 8–12 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Dry on clean raised platforms/tarps 8–12 days to 8–10% moisture (pods snap); never dry on bare soil (aflatoxin risk); store in gunny bags on pallets.
- Marketing: Dry chilli stores 6+ months — sell when price rises (check AgriEasy live prices); graded red dry lots reach exporters; Guntur-type with colour value fetches premium.

## Banana (Kela)
- Season: Planting Jun–Jul (with monsoon) or Feb–Mar (irrigated); tissue-culture plantlets any frost-free month.
- Soil & land prep: Rich loam, good drainage; pit 45×45×45 cm filled with FYM+soil; paired rows 1.8×1.8 m (600 plants/acre, dwarf) — density decides early yield.
- Seed rate & spacing: 600–800 suckers/tissue-culture plants/acre; treat suckers as per label against pseudostem weevil & rhizome rot.
- Irrigation: Drip daily 15–25 L/plant (varies season); banana is water-hungry — critical from shooting to harvest; basin flooding wastes 2× water.
- Fertilizer (per acre): FYM 15–20 t + ~40:20:40 NPK in monthly splits (banana responds to frequent small doses + foliar Zn); stop N at shooting.
- Weeds: Clean cultivation first 4 months; then mulch basins with leaves/trash.
- Pests: Pseudostem weevil (clean field hygiene, remove affected stems), rhizome weevil, aphid (vector of bunchy-top — remove infected plants immediately).
- Diseases: Panama wilt (use tissue-culture + resistant varieties; never plant in wilt history fields), Sigatoka leaf spot, bunchy-top virus.
- Field care: Remove side suckers (keep 1 follower); propping bamboo against winds; cover bunches (perforated polybags) = cleaner bigger fruit +15% price.
- Harvest: 11–14 months; fingers rounded, 3/4 maturity for transport, full for local; harvest with 20 cm crown stem.
- Yield: Average 250–350 q/acre (600 plants × 45–55 kg bunch); good 400–500 q/acre.
- Post-harvest: Cool shade immediately; grade hands by size; wash crown; pack in lined crates; NEVER stack heavy loads on fruit.
- Marketing: Contract with ripeners/wholesale before shooting stage; AgriEasy direct-to-retail lots of graded hands; value-add: chips/flour via SHG for 3× income.
