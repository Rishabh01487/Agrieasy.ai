# Government schemes & farm finance (verify current-year rules on official portals)

Schemes change — details below are indicative; confirm on myscheme.gov.in, the state agriculture department, or Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551 (toll-free, 6 am–10 pm, all days).

## PM-KISAN — income support
- ₹6,000/year per farmer family in 3 installments directly to bank accounts; landholding farmer families only. Check status: pmkisan.gov.in. Only one member per family eligible.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC) — cheapest crop loan
- Crop loans up to ₹3 lakh at subsidized interest (effective ~7%, with prompt-repayment subvention often ~4% — verify current rates with your bank).
- Covers crop + allied (dairy, fishery, poultry). Apply at any bank/cooperative with land record + ID; collateral-free up to ₹1.6 lakh (limit revised periodically — confirm).
- Tip: repay on time every season — the interest saving is real money, and the limit grows with good history.

## PMFBY — crop insurance
- Premium: ~2% of sum insured for kharif, ~1.5% rabi, ~5% commercial/horticulture crops (verify current season terms).
- Insures against drought, flood, hail, pests/disease outbreaks. Enroll via bank/CSC/common service centre BEFORE the cut-off date for your crop — many farmers miss it by days.
- On loss: report within the notified time (usually 72 hours for localized calamities); keep harvest records for yield-based claims.

## Soil Health Card
- Free soil test every 2–3 years with fertilizer recommendation per crop. Contact agriculture department/KVK. Fertilizing by card instead of habit saves ₹1,000–3,000/acre.

## PM-KUSUM — solar pumps
- Up to ~60% subsidy (30% central + 30% state, varies) on solar pumps; extra power can be sold to DISCOM. Apply through state renewable energy nodal agency.

## Micro-irrigation subsidy (PMKSY - Per Drop More Crop)
- 45–55% subsidy on drip/sprinkler for small/marginal farmers (less for others); apply at agriculture/horticulture office; vendor list on state portal.

## eNAM — national online mandi
- Register with mobile+bank at enam.gov.in (or through your mandi/FPO); watch prices across India; sell through your linked local mandi or FPO.

## FPO support (10,000 FPO scheme)
- Farmer Producer Organizations: 300–1,000+ farmer-members; get working capital, grading units, agri-business support. Ask your district agri office or NABARD/kendriya office for nearby FPOs; central scheme supports new FPO formation.

## Other useful things
- PM-GKAY/ration support (household), MGNREGA wages for farm ponds/land works (apply at gram panchayat), subsidized seed mini-kits via ATMA, drone-spray & custom-hiring centres (hire seed drills, laser levelers, harvesters at hourly rates — ask KVK/block office).

## Avoid these money traps
- ⚠️ Cold calls promising "double money in 3 months", deposit-only buyers, unregistered input dealers, fake seed at discount prices. Buy inputs from licensed dealers with bill; verify seed lot labels.
- ⚠️ High-interest informal loans for inputs — KCC exists exactly for this and costs far less.
- ⚠️ Selling without a weight slip or in-app record — disputes are unwinnable without proof.
